Below is the complete document pack to prepare for the HKSTP Incubation application. The mandatory uploads are in **Part F**; several other documents become mandatory only if the corresponding information is declared in Parts A–E.[1]

## Always required

| # | Document | Format / source | Why it is needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Business Plan** | DOC or PDF | Mandatory; must follow the “Notes to Business Plan” in the HKSTP Incubation Programme Guide and align with the 33-month milestone plan. [1] |
| 2 | **Presentation Deck for APM** | PPT, max 30 MB | Mandatory; use it for the Assessment Panel Meeting and make it consistent with the business plan. [1] |
| 3 | **Hong Kong Business Registration Certificate (BR)** | PDF / image | Mandatory; must match the company name and 16-digit BR number in Part A. [1] |
| 4 | **Certificate of Incorporation (COI)** | PDF / image | Mandatory; confirms incorporation date and company details. [1] |
| 5 | **Articles of Association (AOA)** | PDF | Mandatory; must enable full disclosure of shareholders and holdings up to the ultimate top-level shareholder. [1] |
| 6 | **Incorporation Form (NNC1)** | PDF / image | Mandatory; verify shareholders, directors, registered address, and share capital against Parts A and B. [1] |
| 7 | **Bank statements for the past 6 months** | PDF | Mandatory; should support the company’s cash position and Part D financial information. [1] |

## Financial documents

| # | Document | When required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Audited financial statements** | If the company has audited accounts | Upload the latest third-party audited accounts. [1] |
| 9 | **Management accounts** | If the company has been incorporated for less than 18 months | Use instead of audited accounts; ensure revenue, cash, expenses, and funding entries reconcile with Part D. [1] |
| 10 | **Latest Annual Return (NAR1)** | If the company has been incorporated for more than 12 months | Must match the current shareholder and director information. [1] |
| 11 | **Special Resolution** | If applicable | Typically used to authorise the application, representative, or a relevant corporate action. [1] |
| 12 | **Funding evidence** | If external funding is declared in Part D | For each grant, investment, loan, or funding programme: award letter, agreement, bank advice, investor document, or approval notice. [1] |

## Company and shareholders

| # | Document | When required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | **Certificate of Incumbency** | If there is a parent company or corporate shareholder | Supports the parent/subsidiary and ownership declaration. [1] |
| 14 | **Parent company’s Certificate of Incorporation** | If there is a parent company or corporate shareholder | Establishes the parent entity’s legal existence. [1] |
| 15 | **Shareholding structure / corporate-structure chart** | If there are corporate shareholders | Must show ownership up to the ultimate top-level holding company. [1] |
| 16 | **Companies Registry documents for corporate shareholders** | If there are corporate shareholders | Needed to evidence shareholding structure and shareholder identity. [1] |
| 17 | **Shareholder agreement / side letter** | If any exists | Include agreements affecting ownership, control, transfer, voting, conversion, options, warrants, or trust arrangements. [1] |
| 18 | **Trustee / trust documentation** | If applicable | Required for any trust or trustee relationship declared in Part C. [1] |
| 19 | **Convertible note / SAFE / share-option documents** | If applicable | The Part C form expressly asks for these instruments. [1] |
| 20 | **Instrument of Transfer / stamp-duty Bought and Sold Note** | If shares have been transferred | Upload if relevant to the current cap table. [1] |
| 21 | **Board resolution** | If applicable | Use for authorisations or corporate actions relevant to the application. [1] |
| 22 | **Partnership agreement** | If applicable | Upload if the company has a partnership arrangement. [1] |

## Team and employment

| # | Document | When required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23 | **CV for every team member** | Mandatory for listed team members | Use HKSTP’s downloadable CV template; include founders, key management, and key R&D staff. [1] |
| 24 | **ID / passport copy** | If requested or needed to verify identity/work eligibility | Ensure the document number matches Part B. [1] |
| 25 | **Working-visa / employment-visa evidence** | For staff who are not Hong Kong permanent residents | Needed to evidence the legal right to work in Hong Kong. [1] |
| 26 | **Latest MPF record** | If applicable | Listed as an “Other Documents” upload; useful evidence of employment. [1] |
| 27 | **Organisation chart and forecasting** | Mandatory in Part E | Show current and forecast team structure, including technical and non-technical roles. [1] |

## IP, awards, and partnerships

| # | Document | When required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28 | **Award certificate / notification** | For each award listed in Part A or E | Include award name, organiser, date, and evidence of receipt. [1] |
| 29 | **Patent filing / registration evidence** | For each patent listed | Include application number, filing date, applicant name, and status. [1] |
| 30 | **Trademark filing / registration evidence** | For each trademark listed | Ensure applicant name and filing details match the form. [1] |
| 31 | **Registered-design filing / registration evidence** | For each design listed | Upload official filing or registration evidence. [1] |
| 32 | **Technology-licence agreement** | If technology is licensed | Required if the company uses licensed technology; include scope, territory, and term where relevant. [1] |
| 33 | **Partner evidence** | For each partner listed in Part E | LOI, MOU, agreement, email confirmation, or other documentary support. [1] |
| 34 | **Related-party agreement** | If declared in Part C | Required for any commercial relationship with an entity connected to shareholders, directors, or senior management. [1] |

## Priority packing list

For a typical newly incorporated start-up, prepare these files first:

1. `Business_Plan.pdf` or `.docx`
2. `APM_Presentation_Deck.pptx` (under 30 MB)
3. `BR_Certificate.pdf`
4. `Certificate_of_Incorporation.pdf`
5. `Articles_of_Association.pdf`
6. `NNC1_Incorporation_Form.pdf`
7. `Bank_Statements_Last_6_Months.pdf`
8. `Management_Accounts.pdf` (if under 18 months old) or `Audited_Accounts.pdf`
9. `CV_[Name].docx` for every founder and key R&D member
10. `Organisation_Chart_and_Forecast.png/pdf`
11. `Shareholding_Structure.pdf`, if there are corporate shareholders
12. `Funding_Evidence_[Funder].pdf`, for each funding source
13. `IP_Evidence_[Patent/Trademark/Design].pdf`, for each IP item
14. `Shareholder_Agreement.pdf` / `Convertible_Note.pdf` / `Option_Plan.pdf`, if applicable

## Final checks

- Ensure every uploaded document is current, legible, and in the requested format; the application must be signed by an authorised representative.[1]
- Ensure the company has at least two full-time staff legally able to work in Hong Kong, and that founders collectively hold at least 51% of issued shares legally and beneficially.[1]
- Do not rely on HKSTP signing an NDA: submitted materials may be reviewed by HKSTP staff, advisers, and the Assessment Panel.[1]
- If HKSTP requests clarification, respond promptly; a delay exceeding two months causes the application to be treated as withdrawn.[1]
